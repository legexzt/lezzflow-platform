# Saathi Training Script (Field Helper Playbook)

> **Saathi = human helper jo kirana owner ki shop par jaakar LezzFlow Seller app live karwata hai.**
> Script ka target: ek visit (45–60 min) me shop **fully live** — bina rushed lage, bina owner ko confused kiye.

**Golden rules (har Saathi ko yaad rakhna):**

1. Owner ke SAATH karo, owner ke BADLE me mat karo. Wo khud seekhe — tum sirf guide karo.
2. Hindi (ya owner ki bhasha) me baat karo. Jargon nahi: "API", "backend", "deployment" jaise shabd use mat karo.
3. Koi **income/earnings guarantee kabhi mat do.** "Order aayenge, kamai hogi" — ye line banned hai. Sirf itna kaho: *"App aapki dukaan ko online dikhayega, customers aapko phone ki tarah order kar sakte hain."*

---

## Phase 1 — Trust + Setup (10 min)

**Step 1: Introduce karo.**
Apna naam batao, LezzFlow se aaye ho ye batao. Owner ka naam pucho, dukaan kab se hai pucho. 2 minute normal baat — seedha phone mat maango.

**Step 2: Demo dikhao (owner ka phone use karo, apna nahi).**
Play Store se "LezzFlow Seller" app install karwao. Install me dikkat ho to:
- Storage full? Purani photos/videos delete karne me help karo.
- Data off? Hotspot do.

**Step 3: Shop profile banao — owner khud type kare.**
Shop name, address, landmark, opening–closing time. **Time galat mat daalna** — ye baad me orders par asar karega.
*Common mistake:* Saathi jaldi me khud sab bhar deta hai → owner ko kuch pata nahi rehta. Ruko, ungli se dikhao, wo khud tap kare.

---

## Phase 2 — Pehle 20 Products, Barcode Scanner Se (20 min)

**Step 4: Scanner kholo, pehla product scan karo.**
Seller app → "Add Product" → "Scan Barcode". Owner ke haath me phone do — **pehla scan owner khud kare.**

**Step 5: Scan → confirm → price.**
Har product par:
1. Barcode scan karo.
2. Product name sahi aaya? (Agar galat product dikhe → "Wrong product?" dabao, manually search karo.)
3. **Price owner se puchkar daalo** — andaza price kabhi mat daalna.
4. Quantity/unit set karo (packet, kg, litre — jo bhi dukaan me chalta hai).

**Step 6: 20 products ka target.**
Fast-moving items pehle: aata, chawal, tel, cheeni, namak, dal, chai patti, biscuit, soap, shampoo.
*Common mistake:* Saathi khud 20 scan karke "ho gaya" bol deta hai. Rule: **kam se kam 10 scan owner khud kare**, tum sirf 10 me help karo.

**Step 7: Photo check.**
Har product ki photo clear ho — dhundli photo ho to dobara lo. Customer photo dekhkar order karega.

---

## Phase 3 — Mock Order End-to-End (15 min)

**Step 8: Test order banao.**
Apne phone (ya Saathi test account) se Mart app me usi shop se ek chhota order place karo — 2-3 items.

**Step 9: Owner ke phone par order aane do.**
- Notification aayi? **Sound ON hai?** (Bahut se owners ka phone silent hota hai — ye sabse bada missed-order ka reason hai.)
- Owner khud order khole, items padhe.

**Step 10: Accept → Pack → Ready — owner khud kare.**
1. **Accept** dabao.
2. Samaan nikalo, **pack karo** (thaili me daalo — jaise customer saamne khada ho).
3. **"Ready for Pickup"** mark karo.
4. Saathi ke test order ko **cancel/complete** karo taaki system clean rahe.

**Step 11: Owner se repeat karwao.**
"Ab aap bina meri help ke ek baar steps dohrao" — accept, pack, ready. Jab tak wo confident na ho, aage mat badho.

---

## Phase 4 — Close (5 min)

**Step 12: Cheat sheet do.**
`kirana-cheat-sheet-hi.md` ka printout (ya WhatsApp par photo) owner ko do. Samjhao: *"Order aaye to ye kagaz dekho."*

**Step 13: Support number save karwao.**
Owner ke phone me LezzFlow support number save karwao, ek missed call/WhatsApp "hi" bhejkar test karo.

**Step 14: Photo + sign-off.**
Shop ki ek photo (board ke saath), owner ke saath ek photo — ops record ke liye. Owner se pucho: *"Koi sawal?"* — sawal ho to abhi solve karo, "baad me dekh lenge" mat kaho.

---

## Common Mistakes (Saathi galtiyan — inse bacho)

| # | Galti | Sahi tareeka |
|---|-------|--------------|
| 1 | Khud sab kuch karke chale jana | Owner khud kare, tum guide karo |
| 2 | Price andaze se daal dena | Har price owner se confirm karo |
| 3 | Notification sound check karna bhoolna | Test order me sound verify karo |
| 4 | Earnings ka vaada ("roz 20 order aayenge") | **Kabhi mat kaho — banned** |
| 5 | Shop timing galat bharna | Opening–closing owner se confirm karo |
| 6 | 20 products ke baad gayab ho jana | Cheat sheet + support number dekar jao |
| 7 | Owner ka phone silent/vibration par chhodna | Sound ON + notification allowed verify karo |
