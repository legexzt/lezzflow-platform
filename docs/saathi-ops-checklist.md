# Saathi Ops Checklist

> Saathi program ka maksad: **zyada shops nahi, ZINDA shops.** Ek live-active shop > paanch aadhi-onboarded shops.

---

## 1. Recruitment — Local Hi, Local Se

- [ ] Saathi **usi locality/cluster se** recruit karo jahan shops onboard honi hain. Bahar ka banda bhasha, galli, aur dukandaar ka bharosa — teeno me slow hoga.
- [ ] Criteria: smartphone chalana aata ho, Hindi (local bhasha) fluent, 2-wheeler ho to plus. Koi degree/experience zaroori nahi.
- [ ] Pehle din: is repo ka `saathi-training-script.md` padhao + ek senior ke saath **2 live shop visits shadow** karwao. Bina shadowing ke akela mat bhejo.

## 2. Pay Structure — Per Active Shop, Per Hour NAHI

- [ ] Saathi ko **per onboarded-AND-active shop** pay karo — per hour ya per visit nahi.
- [ ] **"Active" ki definition (sabke liye same):**
  1. Go-live visit complete (training script ke saare phases done),
  2. Shop par **kam se kam 10 products** live (price + photo ke saath),
  3. **Ek test/mock order end-to-end** owner ne khud complete kiya (accept → pack → ready).
- [ ] Teenon conditions poori → tabhi "active" count hoga, tabhi payout. Aadhi shop ka aadha paisa nahi — isse quality girti hai.
- [ ] Payout weekly, har active shop ka record (shop photo + owner naam + date) ops sheet me.

## 3. Scope Control — Pehle Cluster Tak Seemit

- [ ] Ek Saathi **ek cluster** (ek locality / 2–3 km radius) ke bahar kaam nahi karega.
- [ ] Naya cluster tabhi kholo jab current cluster expansion criteria meet kare (dekho `expansion-playbook.md`).
- [ ] Ek Saathi par ek saath **max 8–10 active shops** ka load — isse zyada par follow-up mar jata hai.

## 4. Quality Checks — Har Shop Par (visit ke 48 hrs ke andar)

- [ ] **Shop actually live?** Seller app kholo — shop status "Open", timing sahi.
- [ ] **Products real?** Listing me jo 10+ products hain, kya wo dukaan me physically maujood hain? (Random 3 products ka naam pucho — owner turant bata paye.)
- [ ] **Owner khud operate kar sakta hai?** Usse kaho: "Ek order accept karke dikhao." Bina Saathi ki help ke kar le → pass.
- [ ] **Notification sound ON?** Test notification bhejo, awaaz aayi ya nahi verify karo.
- [ ] **Fake entry nahi:** Kisi aur ki dukaan ki photo, ya bina owner ke khud-ba-khud banayi shop = **fraud** → Saathi ka payout us shop ka zero + warning.

## 5. Follow-up Cadence

- [ ] Go-live ke **Day 3** par call: "Order aaya? Koi dikkat?"
- [ ] **Day 7** par visit: products badhao (10 → 20+), naye features samjhao.
- [ ] **Day 14** par review: shop active hai ya dormant? Dormant hai to reason note karo (owner busy? phone issue? interest nahi?).
- [ ] Dormant shops ki list weekly ops review me — 2 hafte dormant = re-visit ya drop.

## 6. Red Lines (Inhe todne par Saathi out)

1. Owner ko **income/earnings guarantee** dena ("roz itne order aayenge", "itni kamai hogi").
2. Owner ke bina permission ke uske phone me kuch install karna ya OTP share karwana.
3. Fake shops ya fake products banana payout ke liye.
4. Customer/owner ka personal data (phone, address) bahar share karna.
